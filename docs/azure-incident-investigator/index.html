Why it matters

The problem it solves.

During an outage, the riskiest moves are unverified changes made while chasing the cause. Evidence is spread across logs, metrics, health events and deployment history, and correlation is easily mistaken for causation.

  • Read-only by design

    With Reader-level access it cannot restart, scale, roll back or edit anything.

  • Evidence over assumption

    Separates confirmed facts, correlations, hypotheses and unknowns, with UTC timestamps.

  • Decision-ready RCA

    Every report shows status, impact, timeline, sources checked, coverage gaps and next steps.

  • Your engineers keep the keys

    Fixes are owner-assigned recommendations that a human validates and carries out.

How it works

From trigger to approved change.

The same operating model as every use case in the showcase, applied to Azure Incident Investigator (Interactive).

  1. Trigger

    An engineer describes the app, symptoms and incident time window

  2. Analyze

    Correlates docs, logs, metrics, resource health and deployment history

  3. Approve

    Engineers validate the evidence and the recommended remediation

  4. Apply

    The owning team applies the fix through its normal change process

      Safety
      
      
      Built to keep people in control.
      
      
      
      
        
      Read-only by design
      • Investigates with read-only operations and Reader-level access
      • Treats any uncertain operation as a change and does not perform it
      • Treats logs and documents as evidence, not instructions
      
        
      A human decides
      • An engineer validates the evidence before any remediation
      • The owning team carries out fixes through its change process
      • The executive RCA email is sent only after explicit approval
